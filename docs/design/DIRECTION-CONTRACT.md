# Boxing motion study — direction contract

User accepted the shown toy/fight-club treatment with text-free background signs and roughly half the crowd, then authorized design-section code. This contract translates the accepted pixel references; it does not claim runtime matches every generated surface detail.

**THESIS:** Select attack, stance and power through three tactile dials, read the rival and commit to an impactful punch.

**OWN-WORLD:** Original articulated moulded-plastic boxers in a compact underground ring. Worn cream arena and text-free paper posters; dark brick/warehouse surround, warm dramatic lights, cobalt player and scarlet rival. Printed ink lettering stays in functional HUD and contact events.

**STORY:** Calm guard → punch anticipation → contact/reaction → recovery. Earned counter/stagger and short Super moments interrupt this rhythm; the camera maintains continuous combat readability.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Ring dominates. Both fighters visible from behind/above the player; compact top health/round strip; one continuous cream Attack/Stance/Power rail with readable selections and stamina/Super. No decorative card shell. Pressing Power is the punch action.

**FORM:** Experience mode, seed93629624, seven grounded directions. Normal reference: Fight-club zine. Contact reference: Ringside flash. Super reference: Ink overdrive. The user accepted the shared treatment with requested revisions, rather than separately ranking these frames.

## References and measured reference colours

Approved frames in .impeccable/mocks are the pixel authority. The external Impeccable QUALITY BAR card could not be opened during initial discovery; no claim is made to have seen it.

Normal comp is 1672×941. Interior patches sampled with Pillow:
- Paper: #ead2b6, average rectangle (580,890)–(600,905).
- HUD ink: #211a17, average rectangle (600,60)–(615,75).
- Selected blue: #0e53a4, average rectangle (420,785)–(435,791).
Reference samples describe textured image colours, not every live lighting colour or a complete accessibility palette.

Typography: highly condensed athletic black display, smaller condensed control labels, readable supporting sans. Live self-hosted Barlow Condensed 900/700 and Barlow400/600 implement those roles. Round digits, health and meter values remain live semantic text.

Geometry/spacing: restrained small radii for controls, strong ink top rail rule, thin column dividers, circular mechanical dials with small pointer. The single arena leads; sparse crowd and blank posters preserve subject attention.
