# Authored articulated fighter assets

The live figures load `public/models/player.glb` and `rival.glb`. Their editable source is `assets/models/boxing-fighters.blend`; `tools/build-fighters.py` builds both original model assemblies with Blender 5.2. The first approved `.impeccable/mocks/01-ringside-flash.png` remains the figure authority. The secondary `02-fight-club-zine.png` supplies quiet guard context. Neither illustration contains an extractable source mesh. Visible forms have been reconstructed; unseen forms are authored interpretations. No third-party character mesh, illustration backplate or new raster texture is shipped as a fighter.

On 2026-10-05, the user authorized continued dedicated character modelling. The first fresh review, `FINISH-REVIEW-AUTHORED-ASSETS.md`, returned REBUILD. This bounded asset batch implements material fixes 1–5 within the existing rig and colour identity. Runtime pose/contact changes and live verification belong to the parent integration packet.

The player hair was rebuilt from hollow C-shaped tubes as 51 closed, fitted sculpt masses: staggered crown/wrap pieces and a dense rear field with a shaped nape edge. Shallow swirl grooves cut into solid crowns, so no curl has an open loop hole. The rival uses six broad, overlapping, closed wedges for a lower asymmetric chestnut quiff and intentional forehead edge. The scalp roots fit the skull; the rival rear remains an interpretation because the primary image does not reveal it.

A continuous dense head mesh contains the eye sockets, brow relief, cheekbones/hollows, shorter angular jaw/chin and nose bridge/tip/wings. The prior separate blade nose was removed. Focused eye apertures, pupils, brows, nostrils and mouth are subdivided prints ray-projected onto the actual tessellated skin, avoiding clipped eyes under the relief. `Mouth`, `BrowL` and `BrowR` remain separate centered reaction meshes. Their rest rotation is identity; the brow slopes are authored in their local geometry. Final glTF local brow centers are approximately `(−0.102, 0.116, 0.194111)` and `(0.102, 0.116, 0.194134)`; the mouth center is `(0, −0.138, 0.196436)`.

The torso now has broader muscular planes and denser sternum/spine sampling, with fitted deltoids, asymmetric biceps/triceps and tapered forearms. Elbow/knee pins are compact and recessed. Trunk legs are narrower, shaped squared shells, extending from native Y −0.135 to +0.49 rather than the previous long slabs. This exposes meaningful thigh length and smaller kneecap/upper-shin transitions. The player retains charcoal mould details; the rival retains cream trunks with fitted red outer panels and the crown waist band.

Boot feet are longer and narrower, with shaped toes, thin soles and continuous ankle transitions. Fitted tall shafts and narrow top bindings attach to `LegLower`; planted feet/soles remain on `Foot`. Cream/red and dark player boot identities are retained. The glove padding has a fitted thumb, mould seams and substantial cream printed cuffs with bound leather edges. Both glove contact landmarks remain local `(0, 0.04, 0.27)`.

The export converts runtime Y-up into Blender Z-up and back to glTF Y-up. `Body` remains at Y 1.46, `Hips` at 1.16 and `Head` at body +0.89. `ArmUpperL/R`, `ArmLowerL/R`, `LegUpperL/R` and `LegLowerL/R` retain normalized native length 1 along Y, proximal end +Y, and the existing IK radial scale. All required group names, `ElbowL/R`, `KneeL/R`, `GloveL/R`, `FootL/R` and `Contact_L/R` are preserved. Export validation found every required node, finite positive segment scales and exact contact coordinates; both GLBs contain no image textures. This is asset-contract evidence, not a combat or smoothness verdict.

Static evaluated meshes are merged by rig parent and material for export, while editable pieces remain independent in the Blender source. `Mouth`, `BrowL` and `BrowR` are kept separate. Merged facial decals retain a `printed` prefix for the runtime shadow policy. Custom corner normals and each parent-local frame are retained. This reduces static mesh surfaces to 46 for the player and 47 for the rival; it does not establish a measured performance target.

Blender exported both GLBs before rendering. The bounded studio verification used two CPU threads, eight samples, 480×600 full-body and 400×400 head frames. All 12 final front/back/three-quarter body/head views were opened at original resolution. They are saved in `docs/design/model-turnarounds/` and copied with provenance to `.impeccable/review/rebuild-asset-{identity}-{view}.png`. The prior submitted studio views were retained as `rebuild-asset-prior-*.png`. Three `rebuild-asset-*-primary-comparison.png` boards place native pixel crops from the first reference beside current studio views without resizing; their sidecars record crop boxes and native dimensions. Different pose, camera and lighting prevent a pixel-identity claim. These are review images only.

The first studio inspection found rear scalp gaps, face-print clipping and an extra upper-shin bulge. One grouped correction added dense rear curls, ray-projected subdivided facial prints, reduced the shin transition and deepened torso sampling; the final confirmation opened all outputs. The resulting player curl spacing is more regular than the primary reference's irregular tumbling locks. The torso remains softer and less visibly panelled than the illustrated back. The rival's neutral sculpt/printed expression differs from the source's contact grimace, and the contact expression requires the parent runtime capture. Studio lighting is deliberately neutral and differs from the ringside highlights. Exact visible character fidelity remains for independent FINISH judgment; this record does not lower the approved authority or self-declare acceptance.

Rebuild/export only with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --threads 2 --python tools/build-fighters.py -- --render=none`. Omit `--render=none` to export and refresh all 12 studio images. Use `-- --render-only --render=heads` for six head studies without overwriting GLBs, or `-- --render=rival` for the rival views. The `.blend` contains both assembled rest rigs, separately editable sculpt pieces, studio lights and the review camera. Applied geometry and merged static surfaces ship in GLB; editable bevel/subdivision modifiers remain in the source.
