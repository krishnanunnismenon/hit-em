## Requested environment revision

All three reference frames were edited: no text on walls, wall posters, spectator placards or other arena scenery, and a visibly reduced crowd of approximately half the original density. Empty paper/paint surfaces retain the warehouse material. Functional HUD and comic combat feedback remain. Exact spectator count is a production scene choice; illustrated crowd density is approximate. Motion is the next design priority; see [the motion contract](MOTION-CONTRACT.md).

## Translation limits

- Generated frames establish composition, material and mood. Browser rendering fidelity and frame rate are unmeasured.
- Decorative slogans, crown motifs, roster identities, timer values and meter values are illustrative. They do not define game rules or branding.
- Implement core text and controls semantically. Fighters, guard tells and attack paths need actual animated 3D geometry rather than a background gameplay illustration.
- Simplify repeated crowd figures, printed surface detail and lighting for the measured laptop budget while preserving the ring's visual hierarchy.
- Keep normal combat quiet enough to read the opponent. Impact lettering belongs near contact and must not cover tells; Super echoes remain local and brief.
- Reduced motion removes camera shake and long colour echoes while keeping hit/guard and selection feedback.
- The calm, counter and Super images are the initial direction review. Entrance, Super ready, pause, victory/loss and responsive layouts still need design after direction selection.
