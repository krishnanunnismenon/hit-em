# Session 04 — First opponent and complete fight flow

## Prompt

Read the product brief and session 03 engine handoff. Create one forgiving opponent with original identity and readable guard/attack tells. Agree its identity and encounter length before inventing campaign content. Opponent decisions should vary without becoming unreadable: guard, telegraph, attack, recovery and occasional openings. Difficulty begins generous and teaches timed defence, situational attacks, stagger follow-ups and power management. Automatic spacing follows the agreed stance model. Keep decisions deterministic under seeded tests and separate from rendering.

Implement ready/start, continuous fight, pause/resume, player/opponent knockout, victory/loss and retry. Use keyboard/pointer controls and the controller input contract. Do not add a roster before one encounter is fun and complete. Ensure losing is recoverable and retry does not replay old hardware input.

## Acceptance

- First encounter can be completed through multiple sensible attack choices.
- Clear recovery opportunities; no unavoidable random attack chains.
- Spam/high power and permanent guarding have fair weaknesses.
- Retry, pause, background loss and hardware reconnect have explicit behavior.
- Fight pacing and stagger time are reviewed with the physical knobs where available.

## Handoff

Opponent patterns/tells, difficulty settings, fight-flow evidence and a list of combat feedback required by session 05.
